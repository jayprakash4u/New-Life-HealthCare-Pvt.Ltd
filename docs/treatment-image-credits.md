# Treatment clinical image credits

These are educational reference photographs and diagnostic images, not New Life patient cases or treatment results. Their inclusion does not advertise that every pictured condition or procedure is treated at the clinic. The source captions identify the conditions shown; the ovarian-mass example retains the source’s uncertainty about a suspected fibroma.

Files were downloaded on 2 November 2026 without retouching or cropping. The ovarian-mass, gastritis, glucose-monitor and blood-pressure files use Wikimedia’s standard 500px thumbnails, as recommended by its image server. The other files use the original downloads. The galleries show the entire image using `object-contain`. Resized image derivatives retain their original license. Source and license links are visible beneath each image. The full source metadata is recorded in `treatment-image-sources.json`.
