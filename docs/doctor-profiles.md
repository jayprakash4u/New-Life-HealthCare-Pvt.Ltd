The six portraits in `public/media/doctors/` are AI-generated illustrations of fictional Nepali physicians, created for this design on November 1, 2026. They are not photographs of New Life clinicians. Cards and portrait alternative text identify the profiles as samples.

Replace the sample names and portraits together with clinic-approved details before presenting these as the actual team. Qualifications, registration numbers, fees and individual experience have not been invented.
