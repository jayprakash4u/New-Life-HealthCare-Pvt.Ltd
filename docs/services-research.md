## Editorial boundaries

### Clinic-owner additions

On October 30, 2026, the clinic owner requested adding Gynecology and checkups for older adults. The website labels these **Gynecology** under Treatments and **Senior citizen health checkups** under Services. Both are included in the appointment selector. This addition is based on the owner's instruction, rather than the previously reviewed Facebook posts. No geriatric-specialist credentials, doctor schedules, prices, or obstetric delivery services are implied.

Educational posts about pregnancy nutrition, kidney health, menstrual breast discomfort, and shingles were visible. An educational topic alone does not establish that a corresponding specialist service is offered. The menstrual breast-discomfort image was inspected directly and is educational content.

The reviewed posts do not establish specialist schedules, current prices, opening hours, 24/7 emergency care, maternity delivery, mammography, cancer treatment, inpatient facilities, or the full range of vaccines. Do not add these as factual claims without further evidence. Flu-vaccine stock and appointment availability can change; retain direct enquiry links.

Facebook presented an additional login prompt while browsing further through the feed. Publicly accessible photo posts remained readable. No account login was performed, and no reviews, comments, or personal details from commenters were used.
